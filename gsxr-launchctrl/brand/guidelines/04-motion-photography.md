## 4.2 Motion Principles

- **Precise cuts** over fades
- **Controlled light sweeps** for reveals
- **Short glitch accents** (1-2 frames) for digital precision
- **Speed ramps** for dramatic emphasis
- **Subtle motion blur** only on high-speed transitions

## 4.3 UI Micro-interactions

| Interaction     | Behavior                              |
|----------------|---------------------------------------|
| Button hover   | Blue underline sweep L→R in 120ms     |
| Status online  | Cyan pulse every 2s                   |
| Terminal typing| 50ms per character, monospace advance |
| Logo reveal   | Blue light sweeps across wordmark    |

## 4.4 Photography Aesthetic

- **High contrast** commercial lighting
- **Wet reflective flooring** in industrial spaces
- **Subtle smoke or haze** for depth
- **Controlled blue highlights** on mechanical surfaces
- **Premium materials**: brushed metal, carbon fiber, glass, leather
