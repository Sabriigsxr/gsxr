# Logo System — GSXR LAUNCHCTRL

## 2.1 Primary Wordmark

The wordmark uses angular geometry with a subtle racing-inspired forward slant (-8°).

```
GSXR
LAUNCHCTRL
```

**Design notes:**
- The **X** in GSXR is the signature element — stylized as intersecting performance bars or a tachometer arc
- Strong horizontal motion through tight letter-spacing
- GSXR in bold condensed display (900 weight); LAUNCHCTRL in slightly lighter weight (700)
- The X can optionally feature a cyan glow or red accent at the intersection

## 2.3 Clear Space

Minimum clear space = height of the 'G' character on all sides.
No text or graphics may intrude into this zone.

## 2.4 Misuse Rules

- Do not stretch or distort proportions
- Do not change colors outside the palette
- Do not add drop shadows or effects
- Do not rotate or skew
- Do not use the wordmark stacked (always horizontal)
- Do not recreate or modify the logo without the original SVG source
