# Photography Generation Prompts — GSXR LAUNCHCTRL

These are detailed prompts for AI image generation tools (DALL-E 3, Midjourney v6, Stable Diffusion XL).
Each prompt produces a specific asset in the GSXR LAUNCHCTRL visual system.

**Global negative prompts (append to every image generation):**
`cartoon, 3d render, drawing, sketch, illustration, text, words, logo, badge, brand, watermark, signature, deformed wheels, duplicated parts, extra limbs, distorted, blurry, low quality`

Structured prompts are in `prompts-set1.json` through `prompts-set3.json`.
