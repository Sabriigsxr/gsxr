## Motion Direction (All Videos)

### Approved Effects

- Precise cuts (no cross-dissolve between scenes)
- Controlled light sweeps (200-500ms duration)
- Speed ramps (2x → 0.5x → 2x for macro shots)
- Short glitch accents (1-2 frames max, 50ms)
- Subtle motion blur on high-speed transitions only

### Effects to AVOID

- Excessive glitch/distortion
- Generic "futuristic HUD" overlays
- "Code rain" / Matrix-style text
- Slow zooms on text
- Over-saturated lens flares
- Generic "tech" spin transitions

### Sound Design Template

```
Layer 1: Cinematic electronic (synthesized pads, low-frequency drones)
Layer 2: Mechanical ambience (engine idle, cooling fans, electrical hum)
Layer 3: Digital UI (keystrokes, interface clicks, data tones)
Layer 4: Impact/transitions (sharp cuts, rising swells)
```

**No copyrighted music.** Use original cinematic electronic composition or licensed stock audio.
